## Relationship to Standard and library

Informational documents do NOT change the Standard's normative content or the library's behavior. They explain, illustrate, or contextualize.

If your document would have to change Standard text or library behavior, escalate to a Standard Track or Library Track Suggestion instead.

## Authority

Informational documents represent the views of the editor body or specific authors, not the Standard. Where authors disagree with one another, multiple Informational documents can coexist (each clearly authored).

The Standard remains the canonical specification. Informational documents are subordinate.
