# Source 1: Owl Labs State of Hybrid Work 2025

**Report:** State of Hybrid Work 2025
**Publisher:** Owl Labs (research partner: Vitreous World)
**Sample:** N=2,000 full-time US workers (18+, companies 2+)
**Collected:** July 2025
**URL:** owllabs.com/state-of-hybrid-work/2025

---

## Workforce Composition
- In-office: 63% | Hybrid: 28% | Remote: 9%
- Managers: 72% of respondents | ICs: 28%
- Private sector: 71% | Public: 26%

## Productivity by Location (Task-Based)
- Focusing: 43% say WFH is most productive
- Creative thinking: 45% WFH most productive
- Meeting deadlines: 38% WFH
- Collaboration: 55% say office is most productive
- Team meetings: 54% office most productive
- Advancing career: 54% WFH (surprising)

## Manager Productivity Assessment
- 69% of managers say hybrid/remote made team MORE productive
- 12% say it reduced productivity
- 19% no change

## Flexibility & Retention
- 40% would start job hunting if flexibility removed
- 22% would demand a pay increase
- 5% would quit outright
- 37% won't accept a job with no flexible hours
- 34% won't accept full-time office requirement

## Costs
- In-office/hybrid daily costs: $55/day average (down from $61 in 2024)
  - Commute $15, Parking $9, Breakfast/Coffee $13, Lunch $18
- Remote daily costs: $18/day (down from $19)
- Hybrid saves $37/day when working from home
- Average commute: 31 minutes each way

## Stress & Wellbeing
- 90% experience workplace stress
- 39% report increased stress vs. 2024
- 27% feeling burnt out
- 24% lack growth opportunities
- 22% not feeling valued

## Meeting Culture
- Average: 5 online + 5 face-to-face meetings/week
- 77% lost time to technical difficulties in hybrid meetings
- Average 6+ minutes lost starting hybrid meetings
- 27% lose 10+ minutes per meeting to tech setup
- 67% have tried to set up video tech and given up

## AI Adoption
- 80% use or experimented with AI (up from 72% in 2024, 49% in 2023)
- 27% use daily | 25% several times/week
- Managers: 90% using AI vs ICs: 55%
- Hybrid workers: 89% | In-office: 80% | Remote: 61%
- 64% report company encourages AI use

## Job Market
- 92% haven't changed jobs in 2025
- 27% actively seeking
- Top reasons: Better pay (49%), Work-life balance (48%), Career growth (44%)

## Workplace Monitoring
- 81% of companies use employee tracking software
- Login/logout: 34% | Meeting count: 28% | Screen/mouse: 21%
- 85% think employers should legally disclose monitoring

## Year-over-Year Trends (2023→2024→2025)
- Working type: In-office 28%→27%→26% | Hybrid 62%→66%→66%
- Daily costs: In-office $51→$61→$55 | Remote $15→$19→$18
- AI adoption: 49%→72%→80%
- Virtual meetings (7-8/week): 2%→6%

## Side Hustles
- 28% have additional jobs/side hustles
- Managers: 31% vs ICs: 19%
- Gen Z & Millennials significantly more likely

## Salary Trade-offs
- Willing to sacrifice for 4-day week: Gen Z 77%, Millennials 75%, Gen X 61%, Boomers 36%
- Willing to sacrifice for flexible hours: Gen Z 78%, Millennials 73%, Gen X 57%, Boomers 29%
