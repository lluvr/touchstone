# Source 3: Staffbase/YouGov Employee Communication Impact Study 2025

**Report:** 2025 International Employee Communication Impact Study
**Publisher:** Staffbase (research partner: YouGov)
**Sample:** N=3,574 employees across 6 countries
**Countries:** Australia (518), Austria (216), Germany (1,067), Switzerland (200), UK (529), US (1,044)
**Survey period:** February 12–21, 2025
**Methodology:** Online interviews via YouGov Panel

---

## Communication Satisfaction
- Coworker relationships: 76% satisfied (highest)
- Vacation/time off: 71% satisfied
- Manager support: 59% satisfied
- Communication quality: 42% satisfied (LOWEST)
- Communication amount: 43% satisfied (LOWEST)

## Non-Desk vs Desk-Based Gap
- Non-desk very satisfied with communication: 9%
- Desk-based very satisfied: 14%
- Non-desk total satisfied: 29%
- Desk-based total satisfied: 47%
- Non-desk rating comms "excellent/very good": 28%
- Non-desk rating "fair/poor": 38%
- Desk-based rating "excellent/very good": 48%

## Turnover & Retention
- 33% cite poor communication as MAJOR leaving factor
- 30% cite it as MINOR leaving factor (total: 63%)
- Germany specifically: 41% major, 29% minor
- "Excellent" communication → 76% "very likely" to stay
- "Poor" communication → 20% "very likely" to stay

## Trust in Information Sources
- Immediate supervisor: 57% trust (highest)
- Company intranet: 51%
- Email/memos from management: 50%
- Company newsletters: 44%
- Employee app: 41%
- Social media: 31% DON'T trust
- Among app users, app trust jumps to 60%

## Primary Information Channels
- Email/memos: 51%
- Immediate supervisor: 47%
- Intranet: 39%
- Company newsletters: 22%
- Employee app: 15%

## Change Communication
- Well-informed about changes: 23%
- Not really/not at all informed: 39%
- Non-desk not informed: 45% vs desk-based: 36%
- Very well informed → 88% happy
- Not at all informed → 36% happy

## Vision & Strategy Clarity
- "Very clear": 20%
- "Somewhat clear": 36%
- "Very unclear": 7%
- Very clear vision → 89% job satisfaction
- Very unclear vision → 25% job satisfaction
- "Easy to understand" comms → 78% rate overall as excellent/very good
- "Not communicated effectively" → 3% rate overall as excellent/very good

## Leadership Communication
- Non-desk never receive senior comms: 12% (UK: 21%)
- Weekly+ senior comms → 77% happy with job
- Never receive senior comms → 41% happy
- Non-desk "well/very well" informed by managers: 48%
- Desk-based: 65%
- Leadership addressing concerns poorly/not at all: 26% (non-desk: 34%)

## Employee Feedback During Change
- Desk-based feedback "yes" considered: 19%
- Non-desk feedback "yes" considered: 12%
- Non-desk feedback "never" considered: 28%
- Employees feeling excluded from change communication: 24%

## Communication Impact on Work
- Productivity impact ("some" or "great"): 63%
- Work motivation impact: 67%
- Understanding vision/mission: 65%

## Crisis Communication
- Rated "excellent/good": 52%
- Employees experiencing gaps: 36%
- Digital screens rated "excellent/good" for crisis: 72% (highest)
- Employees feeling supported: 49% (non-desk: 38%)

## Workplace Loneliness
- Always/often lonely: 10%
- Sometimes lonely: 23%
- Never lonely: desk-based 32%, non-desk 43%
- Employer doing "very good" fostering connections: 20%

---

**Key tension:** Communication is the lowest-satisfaction workplace factor (42-43%), yet has massive downstream impact — 63% turnover link, 64-point satisfaction swing on vision clarity (89% vs 25%), 36-point retention swing (76% vs 20%). Non-desk workers consistently 15-20pp worse across every metric. The gap between communication's impact and its quality is the central finding.
