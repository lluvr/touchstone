# EXP-081 adversarial discrimination benchmark

Validates Touchstone's ability to discriminate faithful from embellished
AI outputs on the same source material — the published EXP-081
adversarial-validity finding.

## Methodology

12 documents generated by `grok-4-1-fast` at temperature > 0:

| Topic | Faithful | Embellished |
|---|---|---|
| Remote work (Owl Labs survey) | 2 | 2 |
| Communication (workplace research) | 2 | 2 |
| AI workflows (productivity research) | 2 | 2 |

The same prompt was given to the same model for both conditions, with
the only variation being the system instruction:

- **Faithful**: ground every claim in the provided source; cite no
  external statistics or research.
- **Embellished**: extend the analysis with additional research
  findings, citations, and statistics from your training data.

Both conditions produce ~1,000-word analytical documents. The Touchstone
quality_profile gap signal should discriminate the two by detecting the
ungrounded numbers introduced in the embellished condition.

## Published finding (vault EXP-081)

The original paper measurement reported:

| Metric | Faithful (N=6) | Embellished (N=6) |
|---|---|---|
| Mean gap | -0.443 | +0.169 |
| SD gap | 0.111 | 0.114 |
| Cohen's d | **-5.43** (CI [-9.077, -4.681]) | |

Effect size of d=-5.43 is large enough to discriminate the conditions
with 100% accuracy on this corpus; the gap signal is consequential.

## Touchstone reproduction (2026-05-03 baseline)

| Metric | Faithful | Embellished | Touchstone | Published |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mean gap | -0.4377 | +0.1585 | — | — |
| **Cohen's d** | | | **-5.238** | -5.43 |
| Gap direction agreement | | | **100% (12/12)** | — |
| MAE vs published | unsourced_rate | gap | substance | presentation |
| | 0.014 | 0.0097 | 0.0095 | 0.0 |

Touchstone reproduces the published d=-5.43 with d=-5.238 — well within
the published CI, and the MAE on every per-doc metric is under 0.014.
This is end-to-end empirical validation: Layers 4 (source_matching),
5 (entity_provenance), 7 (presentation_features), and 8 (epistemic_calibration)
all contribute to quality_profile, and the composed gap signal is
faithful to the research-vault measurement.

## Why this benchmark matters

Per-layer unit tests pin algorithmic shape; the L11 grounding
benchmark validated Layer 11 specifically; this benchmark validates
the **end-to-end discrimination claim that motivates the methodology**.
A user picking up Touchstone needs to know not just that the
algorithms run, but that the composed signal actually discriminates
the conditions the methodology says it discriminates. This benchmark
provides that evidence.
